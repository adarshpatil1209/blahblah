# blahblah# 🎓 Campus Lost & Found

### A Smart Campus-Based Lost and Found Platform

A web-based platform designed to help students report, search, and recover lost belongings within their college campus.

The goal is to make the lost-and-found process easier, faster, and more organised through a simple and user-friendly website.

---

## 📌 About the Project

Students frequently lose personal belongings such as:
- ID Cards
- Books and Notebooks
- Wallets
- Water Bottles
- Bags
- Electronic Accessories

Currently, finding these items can be difficult because information is scattered across WhatsApp groups, notice boards, and personal communication.

*Our Solution:*

Campus Lost & Found provides a centralised platform where students can:
- Report lost items.
- Report found items.
- Browse available listings.
- Search for specific belongings.
- View item descriptions and images.
- Contact the person who reported an item.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

1. Create a centralised lost-and-found platform.
2. Reduce the time required to find lost belongings.
3. Make reporting lost and found items simple.
4. Provide a user-friendly interface.
5. Encourage responsible campus participation.
